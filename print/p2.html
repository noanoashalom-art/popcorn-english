Smartphones and Sleep

סמארטפונים ושינה

1 Today, almost everyone has a smartphone. People use their phones to talk, study, shop, and watch videos. However, many people also use their phones in bed, just before they go to sleep. Scientists say that this habit can affect the quality of our sleep.

2 There are two main reasons why phones cause sleep problems. The first reason is the blue light from the screen. This light makes the brain think that it is still daytime. The second reason is that phones keep the mind active. Messages, games, and social media make it hard to relax.

3 Blue light reduces the amount of melatonin in the body. Melatonin is a natural hormone that helps us fall asleep. When there is less melatonin, people need more time to fall asleep, and they often wake up during the night.

4 Poor sleep has many negative results. People who do not sleep enough often feel tired and nervous during the day. They also make more mistakes at work and at school. In the long term, lack of sleep can increase the risk of health problems, such as heart disease and diabetes.

5 Teenagers and young adults are the group that is affected the most. A recent study found that students who used their phones in bed slept about one hour less than students who did not. It appears that young people find it especially difficult to put their phones away.

6 Experts suggest several simple solutions. For example, people can stop using screens one hour before bedtime. They can also turn on night mode, which reduces blue light, or leave the phone in another room. Reading a paper book instead of a screen is another good idea.

7 In conclusion, smartphones are useful tools, but they can damage our sleep. By changing a few habits, people can sleep better and feel healthier.
