Social Media and Young People

רשתות חברתיות וצעירים

(1)Researchers study how social media affects young people. (2)Some studies suggest that using social media for many hours can lead to stress and poor sleep. (3)On the other hand, social media can also help young people stay in contact with friends. (4)In conclusion, the effect of social media depends on how people use it.
