Working While Studying

עבודה בזמן הלימודים

(1)Many college students work while they study. (2)There are advantages and disadvantages. (3)One advantage is that students earn money. (4)Another advantage is that they gain experience. (5)However, the main disadvantage is that they have less time to study. (6)As a result, some students get lower grades.
