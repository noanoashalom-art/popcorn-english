Clean Water in Cities

מים נקיים בערים

(1)Cities need clean water. (2)In the past, many cities had serious problems because the water was not clean. (3)This caused many diseases. (4)Today, most cities use special methods to clean water. (5)For example, they filter the water and add chemicals that kill bacteria. (6)As a result, people in these cities are healthier. (7)However, in some parts of the world, clean water is still a problem.
