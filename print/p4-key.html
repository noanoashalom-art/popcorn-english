Comprehension Questions

  • 1.
    two of: a short comment / a demonstration / a question
  • 2.
    Tamar hears only “You played badly.” Amir gets specific advice: his passes were excellent and he should look up before he shoots.
  • 3.
    Because it is specific: it tells the player which skill worked.
  • 4a.
    A new player needs to improve many things: choose one or two important points first
  • 4b.
    A player is in the middle of a game: watch, write down a few points, and talk about them after the game
  • 4c.
    Players are doing a training exercise: give a quick correction right away
  • 5.
    Because their advice must be respectful and kind.
  • 6.
    self-evaluation
  • 7.
    FALSE – “If coaches only give advice and move on to the next game, players may never use it. Good advice should lead to more practice.”
  • 8.
    Because some players feel embarrassed when the coach talks about their mistakes in public.
  • 9.
    d) Good coaches give clear advice and a chance to improve.
